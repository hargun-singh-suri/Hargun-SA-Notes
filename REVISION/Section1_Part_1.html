System Requirements & Architectural Drivers

Software Architecture & Design of Large-Scale Systems — Michael Pogrebinsky

1 · System Design & Architectural Drivers

Architectural drivers are the few things that most influence how you build a system — mainly business goals, required quality attributes, and constraints. Example: if the business goal is "support 10 million users," that single driver pushes you toward a horizontally-scalable design from day one.

2 · Feature Requirements — Step-by-Step

  1. Gather feature requirements from stakeholders
  2. Identify the quality attributes needed
  3. Note down constraints
  4. Turn all of it into an architecture
03

Quality Attributes Requirements

  • A quality attribute is a measurable property of the system — like "how fast" or "how often it stays up" — not a feature
  • The architecture you pick directly controls how good each quality attribute can be
Quality Attribute Checks
Testabilitymust be provable, not just claimed
Trade-Offse.g. more security = slower speed
Feasibilitycan budget & timeline actually deliver it?
04

System Constraints

  • A constraint is a decision already made for you before design starts — you must design around it, not fight it
  • Keep the architecture loosely coupled so that if a constraint changes later, you don't have to rebuild everything
System Constraints
Technicale.g. must reuse an existing database
Businesse.g. fixed budget or deadline
